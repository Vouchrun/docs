### Usage rules

- **Orange is the primary action, blue is the secondary action.** Use orange for the single main action in a view (Stake, Apply, Accept) and the current selection; use blue for supporting actions, **Connect Wallet** and links to external records. Never place an orange and a blue button side by side as equal choices.
- **Text and icons on orange or blue are always dark** (`#111111` in the dark theme, `#1B1B1F` in the light theme) — never white.
- **In the light theme, orange and blue are fill colours only.** For orange or blue text use the darker shades `#A3480B` (orange) and `#2563C9` (blue).
- **Use orange sparingly** — the one primary action, the current selection and the single most important figure.
- **Buttons are pill-shaped** (`border-radius: 999px`).
